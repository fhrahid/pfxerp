# Authorization roles and responsibilities

**Scope:** source-level behavior at the audited revision. `IMPLEMENTED` means a route and its relevant service check exist; it does not assert deployed behavior. CHRO and multi-label ABAC are target decisions, not current capabilities. The owner named six primary perspectives, despite calling them five. Sources: `docs/srs/DECISIONS.md:5–12`, `prisma/schema.prisma:14–21`, `src/common/types.ts:10–24`.

## Shared identity and authorization model

**IMPLEMENTED:** `User.role` holds one string; `Role` stores a name, description and `isSystem`, without a permission relation. `SquadMembership.role` separately identifies `PM` or `DEVELOPER` within a squad. The system seeds CEO, CFO, CTO, CPO, PM and DEVELOPER, with no CHRO. A custom role can be created but gains no general endpoint permissions. `JwtStrategy.validate` reloads the active user and active squad IDs; the global `RolesGuard` checks `@Roles` strings and permits CEO through every decorated role check. A route with no `@Roles` is available to any authenticated user unless its handler/service checks ownership. Sources: `prisma/schema.prisma:237–250`, `:342–355`, `:1042–1052`; `src/modules/roles/roles.service.ts:9–20`, `:70–102`; `src/modules/auth/jwt.strategy.ts:29–47`; `src/app.module.ts:78–83`; `src/common/guards/roles.guard.ts:15–33`.

**PLANNED (owner decision):** CHRO and CPO are distinct; a person can hold multiple roles; CEO can assign and revoke any existing or new role; authorization becomes ABAC with deny by default. A job-role label grants no service capability; explicit service/action grants and resource predicates are required. The CEO directly grants/revokes per-person service actions by owner decision; grant-scope limits are UNVERIFIED. `docs/srs/DECISIONS.md:7–8`.

## Data visibility vocabulary

`own` means records belonging to or created/assigned to the caller; `assigned squad` means a currently active squad membership; `organization-wide` means the query is not narrowed by caller or squad; `restricted` means a named role or state gate, possibly combined with one of the preceding scopes. These describe observed query behavior, not owner-approved future entitlements. Examples: own wallet (`src/modules/ledger/ledger.controller.ts:165–185`), squad tasks (`src/modules/tasks/tasks.service.ts:413–455`), organization-wide clients (`src/modules/clients/clients.service.ts:28–32`), restricted executive logs (`src/modules/audit/executive-audit.controller.ts:13–20`).
