# Authorization gaps, ownership and approval boundaries

This file distinguishes **IMPLEMENTED** enforcement at source level from **PLANNED** owner decisions and **UNVERIFIED** business policy. A missing guard or filter is a source-backed exposure, not an approval of that access. The matrix in [rbac-matrix.md](rbac-matrix.md) is the route/resource index.

## Segregation-of-duties rules to preserve or decide

**IMPLEMENTED:** pool funding requester cannot approve own request (`src/modules/ledger/ledger.controller.ts:198–215`); PM cannot verify own assigned task, and CTO technical review checks active membership conflict (`src/modules/tasks/tasks.service.ts:546–685`); proposed fine needs CFO approval (`src/modules/payroll/payroll.service.ts:31–112`); loan disbursement needs CEO-approved governance record (`src/modules/loans/loans.service.ts:89–119`). These are per-workflow checks; they are not a general SoD engine.

**PLANNED:** when one person has several roles, authorization shall evaluate actor identity as well as roles, so role combinations cannot defeat same-record maker/checker constraints (`docs/srs/DECISIONS.md:7–8`). **PROPOSED:** prohibit the same actor from requesting, approving and executing a material payment unless a separately documented emergency rule applies. The thresholds, substitute approvers and emergency procedure are **UNVERIFIED**.

## Business-policy decisions still required

1. After assigning payroll approval to CHRO, who may **submit, reject and execute disbursement**? Does CEO retain an emergency override, and does it require a different actor? Current CPO/CEO behavior is in `src/modules/payroll/payroll.service.ts:125–267`, `:318–327`.
2. Which current CPO powers move to CHRO (staff account creation/update, role assignment, squad transfers, fines, notices), and which product/client decisions remain with CPO? See `src/modules/users/users.controller.ts:91–136`, `src/modules/squads/squads.controller.ts:91–175`, `src/modules/clients/clients.controller.ts:70–119`.
3. May CEO perform CFO operational actions, including payout execution and equity investment? If so, how should recipients and maker/checker conflicts be handled? See `src/modules/sprint-payouts/sprint-payouts.service.ts:108–132`, `src/modules/equity/equity.service.ts:19–29`.
4. Who may see organization-wide client, citation, bounty and consultancy data, and who may export it? Existing open reads are evidenced in GAP-02 and GAP-11.
5. Is CEO board sign-off valid before the required board vote count, and can an internal employee record a client's acceptance without client-side proof? See `src/modules/governance/governance.service.ts:96–120`, `src/modules/clients/clients.service.ts:151–166`.
